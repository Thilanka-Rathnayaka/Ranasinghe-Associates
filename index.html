About Us

Trusted Financial Partners Since 2015

Ranasinghe & Associates is a leading professional services firm in Sri Lanka, specializing in assurance, accounting, and tax services. Under the leadership of Managing Partner P. Ranasinghe, we have built a reputation for excellence, integrity, and client-focused solutions.

With offices in Galle and Colombo, we serve businesses of all sizes across Sri Lanka, providing comprehensive financial services that help clients achieve their objectives and maintain regulatory compliance.

What We Offer

Our Services

Comprehensive financial solutions designed to meet every business need and drive sustainable growth

Income Tax Services

Expert planning and preparation of individual and corporate income tax returns, ensuring full compliance with Sri Lankan Inland Revenue regulations while optimising your tax position.

Value Added Tax (VAT) & All Other Tax Services

End-to-end VAT registration, return filing, and compliance management. We also handle all other applicable taxes — NBT, WHT, and more — so you remain fully up to date.

Audit & Assurance Services

Comprehensive statutory and internal audits, compliance reviews, and assurance engagements to ensure accuracy, transparency, and confidence in your financial reporting.

Financial Statement Preparation

Preparation of accurate, SLFRS/LKAS-compliant financial statements — including balance sheets, income statements, and cash flow reports — tailored to your business size.

Bookkeeping Services

Accurate, timely day-to-day bookkeeping and maintenance of your financial records so you can stay focused on running and growing your business with full confidence.

Financial Consulting

Strategic advisory on business planning, financial analysis, risk management, and investment decisions to help you make informed choices and achieve long-term success.
